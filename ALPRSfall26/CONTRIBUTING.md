# Project contribution notes

The repository-level [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) governs public
release, redaction, and Drive handling. This project file adds the research
review requirements.

Before adding a source, record what it is, who issued or published it, its
date, how it was obtained, and why it belongs in the project. Do not present a
paraphrase as a holding. Add a page or paragraph pinpoint when a legal claim
has been independently read and verified.
